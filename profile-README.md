# Ahmed Mujtaba

**AI researcher — PhD student at [Silicon Austria Labs](https://silicon-austria-labs.com/) and the [University of Innsbruck](https://www.uibk.ac.at/), Austria.**

I work on making deep learning run — and learn — on hardware that barely has room for it. My doctoral
research is on **model-heterogeneous federated learning** for edge devices: letting vehicles, sensors and
microcontrollers train collaboratively when every device runs a different model architecture.

**Website → [ahmed-mujtaba-98.github.io](https://ahmed-mujtaba-98.github.io/)**

---

### Research interests

- **Federated learning & distillation** — model-heterogeneous collaborative learning, knowledge distillation, non-IID data
- **EdgeAI & TinyML** — inference and on-device training on Jetson, STM32 and ARM Cortex-M class hardware
- **Model compression** — quantization, pruning, hardware–software co-design
- **Applied computer vision** — driver and in-cabin monitoring systems for automotive

### Background

Before the PhD I was an AI Researcher at **DeltaX Co., Ltd.** in Seoul, building end-to-end deep learning
pipelines for automotive applications with Hyundai-Mobis and KIA, deployed on NVIDIA Jetson, TI TDA4X and
HAILO. Earlier, as a Graduate Research Assistant at the **ISML Lab, Gachon University**, I worked on TinyML —
CNN quantization and efficient inference on ARM Cortex-M using CMSIS-NN and CMix-NN.
