About

I am a Junior Scientist and PhD Student at Silicon Austria Labs GmbH and the University of Innsbruck, Austria, where my doctoral research centres on lightweight collaborative learning for edge devices. My work sits at the intersection of efficient machine learning, embedded systems, and distributed intelligence — spanning neural network quantization, pruning, knowledge distillation, and federated learning tailored to resource-constrained hardware.

Prior to my PhD, I worked as an AI Researcher at DeltaX Co., Ltd. in Seoul, collaborating with Hyundai-Mobis and KIA on end-to-end deep learning pipelines for automotive applications including Driver Monitoring Systems and In-Cabin Monitoring Systems. This industry experience gave me hands-on expertise in hardware-software co-design and the deployment of optimised models on embedded platforms such as NVIDIA Jetson, Texas Instruments TDA4X, and HAILO. Earlier, as a Graduate Research Assistant at ISML Lab, Gachon University, I conducted foundational research on TinyML, publishing peer-reviewed work on CNN quantization and efficient inference on ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers.

Research Interests

EdgeAI

Deploying intelligent models directly on edge devices with minimal latency and power consumption.

TinyML

Running machine learning inference on microcontrollers and ultra-constrained embedded systems.

Collaborative Learning on the Edge

Enabling multiple edge devices to learn jointly without centralising sensitive data.

Federated & Distributed Learning

Privacy-preserving distributed training across heterogeneous devices and non-IID data distributions.

Neural Network Quantization

Reducing model bit-width to 8-bit and sub-byte precision while preserving accuracy.

Pruning & Knowledge Distillation

Structured sparsity and teacher-student learning to compress models for real-time edge inference.

Embedded Computer Vision

Image classification, object detection, and driver/cabin monitoring systems on embedded hardware.

Hardware-Software Co-design

Co-optimising DL models and deployment pipelines across NVIDIA Jetson, TI TDA4X, HAILO, and STM32 platforms.
